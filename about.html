About

Boutique when it should be. Twenty trucks when it has to be.

Crew on set

Astro Cat has spent decades behind the camera and in the truck. One company, two ways of working: Studio for stories that are written, shot and cut, and Live for shows that happen once and have to be right the first time.

The crew scales to the job. Some briefs need a lean boutique team that moves fast; others need a full multi-department operation. The attention to service and detail stays the same at either size.
