Before you write

Can one job use both Studio and Live?

Yes. Plenty of shows need a live broadcast and a finished film out of the same day. Tick “Not sure” and tell us what you need to end up with.

Do you work outside Los Angeles?

Yes. We’re based in LA and travel with the crew and kit the job needs.

What should go in the brief?

What it is, who it’s for, where and when, and a budget range if you have one. Rough is fine. We’ll ask the rest.
